Maritime safety disclaimer

The apps in the Helmology series, including Helm.Warning and Helm.Ports, are informational tools intended to support situational awareness. They do not replace any official source of maritime safety information.

Under the International Convention for the Safety of Life at Sea (SOLAS) Chapter V, the master's obligation to consult official navigational warnings remains with official channels: Notices to Mariners, NAVTEX, SafetyNET, ECDIS updates and the relevant national hydrographic offices. Warnings displayed by these apps may be incomplete, delayed or out of date. Always verify against official publications before making any operational decision.
