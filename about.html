About the developer

Alfred Panakkal Jose is a full-stack developer and a serving Third Officer with a tanker background. He writes software for the problems he actually meets at sea — the Helm series exists because the tools were missing on the chart table, not because a roadmap asked for them.

Helm.Warning came from checking NAVAREA warnings across dozens of national websites. Helm.Ports came from wanting the NGA World Port Index to be searchable, filterable and connected to tides and weather. Every app in the series follows the same rule: build for the watch keeper first.

Helmology is the home of the series. Recruiters and collaborators are welcome to reach out via the links below.
